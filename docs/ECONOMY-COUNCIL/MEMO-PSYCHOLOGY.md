## 0. Role & mandate — and the word "اعتیادآور"

My job is the emotional engine: make players **want to return daily** and
**want to hold as identity**, without ever manufacturing compulsion.

The owner's word «اعتیادآور» (addictive) is translated here into one lawful
design goal: **a high-frequency RETURN loop built on identity, mastery, and
ritual — not a compulsion loop built on money-loss schedules.** The science of
"can't stop playing" (variable reward, loss aversion, near-miss) is real and we
use it — but always attached to *skill and shared experience*, never to spend
pressure, never to variable-ratio money mechanics, and always under the honest
framing LAW 4 demands. Compulsion that harms the player eventually harms the
product: trust is the only durable retention asset a testnet game owns today.

Verified emotional raw material (facts, not invention): one shared worldwide
level per UTC day · the scoreboard is unbuyable (LAW 1) · deaths are public
ghosts/cards · the vault sits at the peak wick everyone can SEE · the meter
(read 3.94% of 4.0 ETH on 2026-10-07) is a public collective climb · 254,293
WICK already burned per the live feed line · the airdrop rehearsal page lets
anyone verify a would-be share. That is an unusually honest psychological
toolbox — nearly everything we need already exists as *true*.

## 1. VERDICTS — the owner's four questions

**Q1 — Bridge product → buy page: which placements convert, which betray trust?**
VERDICT: convert at **pride moments with identity blocked, money one click away**: (a) the vault gate at the peak (strongest), (b) the post-death panel ("your run builds weights" → link, then buy), (c) the /airdrop rehearsal board (endowment preview). These work because desire already exists and the CTA completes an identity, not a trade.
BETRAYS TRUST: any CTA near the leaderboard or inside score submission (reads as pay-to-rank), pre-first-play entry overlays, mid-run interruptions, price/ROI framing, countdown urgency that isn't real. Rule: **buy CTAs live only where the player just DID something and wants to keep the feeling.**

**Q2 — Should PLAY require holding?**
VERDICT: **NO — never, and not only because LAW 1.2 says so.** Psychologically, gating play destroys the competence engine that makes status worth wanting: an unbuyable scoreboard is precisely why climbing it means anything. Gating also converts curious newcomers into resentful non-players at peak curiosity. Commitment & consistency works better in reverse: free daily play → identity forms → buying becomes identity-consistent behavior. Tier-gated NON-access perks (vault, trails, badge lane, later event-entry) deliver the same commitment signal at zero onboarding cost. Gate the *expression* of belonging, never the game.

**Q3 — The need chain: first-buy → more buys → continuous hold?**
Chain: *"I am a climber"* (free, earned daily) → *"climbers who hold carry fire"* (visible cosmetic identity: vault/ember trail/badge) → *"my play now binds to a ledger that will matter"* (weights via linked wallet) → *"holding is how I stay that person"* (identity-consistency + daily re-armed vault) → post-grad: *"my own token's chart is a level"* (MIRROR).
EXISTS TODAY: competence/mastery (strong), curiosity/near-miss (strong design, quiet delivery), honest loss aversion (streak/lock).
MUST BE BUILT: **belonging rituals**, **visible holder identity**, **collective-progress celebration**, **endowment preview** — the relatedness and status layers are thin.

**Q4 — Honest heart-audit of the current loop + v2 outline?**
Feels thin: (1) the vault near-miss is SILENT — dying below the peak teaches nothing, no "you were 3 candles from the vault" moment; (2) holder status is barely visible to others; (3) the prediction loop closes without ceremony — a text line, no reveal, no community overlay; (4) the burn feed is a storyless number; (5) one missed day flattens a streak to 1 with no mercy narrative, and the day-10 cap makes late days emotionally flat; (6) copy bug: death panel says "graduation: 5 eth class" while the reconciled target is 4.0 ETH — a LAW 4 trust leak to fix.
v2 cycle: **PLAY** (shared mountain) → **FEEL** (near-miss, verdict, rank) → **EXPRESS** (death card, duel, rival tag) → **BELONG** (link wallet, overlay, ritual hour) → **IDENTIFY** (hold → trail → vault streak → season badge) → **RETURN** (tomorrow's locked call + tomorrow's terrain). Every arrow must be one click.

## 3. Emotional ladder design — "want to hold, want to return"

Two parallel ladders, deliberately separated (LAW 1):

- **SKILL LADDER** (public, unbuyable, LAW 1): daily rank, streak, PB, duel record. Holding can never touch it. This ladder is why the game is credible.
- **IDENTITY LADDER** (cosmetic/weights only, LAW 2): everything below. Expression of belonging, never advantage.

### Identity ladder rungs (proposed; cosmetics/routes/archive/weights only)

| Rung | Name | Requirement | Player feels | Lawful expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Climber | nothing (walletless) | "I'm in" | name on today's board; death cards; full game |
| 1 | Linked | wallet connected, 0 WICK | "I'm counted" | weights ledger binds to identity; subtle chain-stitch profile mark |
| 2 | Ember | hold ≥1,000 WICK [PROVISIONAL tier] | "I carry fire" | vault opens on peak days; ember trail for the day; holder badge lane (spec §5.4) |
| 3 | Flamekeeper | participation: N vault opens / season streak | "my devotion is visible" | named trail variants + season badge (participation-earned cosmetics — holding alone never graduates this rung) |
| 4 | Gold | hold ≥100,000 WICK [PROVISIONAL tier] | "I'm part of the mountain's foundation" | gold trail; distinct frame; **profile-level only — never rendered on the leaderboard** |

Design laws for the ladder: status flows from **participation + membership**,
not from spend size alone (Rung 3 exists precisely so a holder with 1,001 WICK
who shows up daily outranks a silent whale in *identity* terms, while the whale
gets gold *color*). Whales are honored, never worshipped; free climbers are
respected, never pitied. No rung changes score, physics, or access — ever.

### Named trails & season badges
- Trails are the single best endowment carrier: they are seen in motion (in your own run and in ghosts), they expire daily unless re-earned, and re-earning is *playing*, not paying.
- Season = one "ascent" (proposed 30 UTC days). End-of-season: badge on profile ("Season 1 · 22 days · flame x9"), cosmetic trail variant for the next season. Badges are autobiography — a visible history of showing up.
- Season reset touches **cosmetics and streaks only**, never weights already banked in the ledger (weights accrue per event log; the ledger is read-only truth — GLOSSARY-FA "دفتر ثبت وزن‌ها").

## 4. First-buy moment spec — where "get $WICK" converts

**The emotional state to engineer:** *"I did the hard thing. The reward is
visible, blocked only by membership, membership is one honest click away — and
nothing about my score changes either way."* Desire → blocked → cheap honest
path → autonomy preserved. That is the whole conversion, in one sentence.

**Two-step conversion (foot-in-door, honest):** micro-commit first — CONNECT
(free, reversible, unlocks weights binding) — then BUY (identity completion).
Never jump a cold player straight from play to a purchase CTA.

- **Moment A — THE VAULT GATE (primary).** Player reaches the peak wick holding <1,000 WICK: today the button simply doesn't appear — the strongest near-miss in the product is silent. Spec: render the vault as visible-but-locked AT the peak, with one CTA: `THE VAULT OPENS FOR HOLDERS · 1,000 $WICK MINIMUM · [GET $WICK] · [NOT TODAY]`. Copy under LAW 4: "Holders open the vault. Trail today: ember. Testnet." No value promise, no countdown, exit honored. Secondary placement: the death panel below the peak — `YOU WERE {n} CANDLES FROM THE VAULT` (honest near-miss telemetry, already computable from `candlesPassed` vs `peakIdx`).
- **Moment B — POST-DEATH PRIDE.** Right after a good run (death/graduated panel) is the pride peak: `THIS RUN BUILDS AIRDROP WEIGHTS — LINK YOUR WALLET TO COUNT IT` → then, only after linking, the ember line. Buying CTA appears at rung-1, not at rung-0.
- **Moment C — THE LOCK.** After "CALL LOCKED — SCORES TOMORROW VS THE REAL CANDLE": `CALLS BUILD WEIGHTS WHEN YOUR NAME IS LINKED` — the open loop plus the free step.
- **Moment D — ENDOWMENT PREVIEW.** /airdrop board: for any identity with weights, `IF THE SNAPSHOT WERE TODAY, YOUR LEDGER SHOWS {N} WEIGHTS — LINK A WALLET TO BIND THEM` (computed from the real ledger; the rehearsal board is exactly this — honest by construction, no invented numbers).
- **Copy that works under LAW 4:** verbs of building/carrying/opening ("builds weights", "holders open the vault", "carry the ember trail") + plainly stated testnet + "nothing guaranteed". **Copy that breaks trust:** earn/guaranteed/profit, price talk, "last chance", fake countdowns, any suggestion that holding affects rank.

Where else: nowhere. Two buy-adjacent surfaces max per session; the game itself
stays a commerce-free mountain.

## 5. Repeat/hold rituals — keeping holding identity-relevant weekly (not just day 1)

Why holding fades without ritual: day 1 buying = novelty + altruism; week 2 =
"why am I holding a testnet token?" The answer is that **the vault re-arms
every single day** — holding is not a one-time key, it is a daily membership in
the one mechanic at the top of the mountain. Make that membership *visible and
countable*:

- **Vault streak (cosmetic):** consecutive days opening the vault → flame progression (ember → brighter ember → gold-tinged at N opens, names earnable — "Flamekeeper"). Score-blind, wallet-gated, participation-shaped. Holding becomes a *daily identity performance*, and the death panel shows yesterday's flame state.
- **Weekly cadence (sketch, UTC):**
  - **Mon — "The Week's Terrain":** which famous archive days return this week (narrative scheduling); practice marathon framing.
  - **Wed — Duel Day:** ghost races & rival tags ritualized; death-card repost hour on X (owner-only account — cards designed for the owner to amplify, no owner work required beyond posting).
  - **Fri/Sat — THE REVEAL:** all of today's locked predictions overlaid on the real candle (H5's dormant social-proof layer): "the community drew this; the market drew that" — one image, one cliffhanger, the weekly relatedness spike.
  - **Daily 20:00 UTC — Ritual Hour (optional):** announced collective play window for today's level. Appointment + synchrony ("we climb together") is the cheapest relatedness multiplier in existence; no code beyond a posted time.
- **Season calendar sketch:** 30-day Ascent → Week 1 "sett-in" (streak building) · Week 2 duels · Week 3 reveal-marathon · Week 4 "summit week" (vault double-visibility events) → Season badge ceremony → 48h inter-season calm (deliberate rest window — see §6) → next Ascent.
- **Collective progress days:** meter milestones (every +25% toward 4.0 ETH) = announced community climb day. The meter is public truth on the token page — celebrating real milestones is honest FOMO: *"the mountain fills because people like you keep climbing."*
- **Guild death-cards:** blocked on owner (guild creation is owner-only, frozen) — design the card templates now so the day guilds exist, the ritual ships. Ghost races and rival tags carry the social load until then.
- **Post-grad, holding deepens:** MIRROR (H6) makes the token's own chart playable — the holder's balance history literally becomes terrain; burn counter gives the hold a visible public cause. These are the strongest identity-consistency locks in the roadmap; pre-grad we build the habit shape they will snap into.

## 6. Player-protection section (required) — what I refuse, what we keep

**The owner asked for "اعتیادآور"; I deliver "unmissable daily", not "can't
stop".** The difference is a design line, and it is drawn here.

**I refuse to design:**
1. **Variable-ratio money-loss loops** — lootboxes/gacha/spin mechanics purchasable with ETH or tokens; any reward schedule whose *primary* variable is how much you spend.
2. **Dark patterns** — fake countdowns, hidden fees, confirm-shaming ("No thanks, I hate rewards"), pre-checked purchases, guilt-framed CTAs, fabricated scarcity ("only 3 vaults left!").
3. **Targeting compulsive spenders** — whale-exclusive escalation ladders designed to create spend pressure; any "spend more to keep up" mechanic; price/ROI-flavored retention hooks.
4. **Score-adjacent monetization** — anything that makes money smell like rank (LAW 1 red-line; also the fastest trust-killer).
5. **Streak terror** — the streak already uses loss aversion; it must stay proportionate. I will not add punishment stacking, public shaming of broken streaks, or streak-insurance sales.
6. **Humiliation styling of non-holders** — holder cosmetics are visible; they never insult the free climber (no "peasant" aesthetics, no locked-mode taunts). The free player is the mountain's population; without them the mountain is empty.

**Healthy-engagement structures ALREADY in place — keep them, never raise them
as a retention lever:**
- Daily caps: 1 vault/day · 1 prediction lock/day · practice cap 2/day · streak weight cap 10/day · 600/wallet snapshot cap. These bound daily effort by design — they are the difference between a habit and a grind, and they make caps *fair* (a whale cannot out-grind you).
- No energy systems, no pay-to-continue: skill retries are unlimited and free — appropriate for a platformer; the daily board reset naturally bounds chasing.
- Honest clocks: one real UTC day, announced snapshot timing, "nothing guaranteed", testnet stated plainly — FOMO in this game must always be *true* (the day really ends; the lock really is one per day; the meter really reads 3.94%).

**Watch items (recommend for the KPI panel, §7):** session-length tails and per-player retry counts (skill-chasing vs frustration-rage); a 48h inter-season calm window each cycle; timezone clarity on streak resets (a "missed because midnight" apology culture is a churn factory — show next-reset time in the streak UI).

**One trust leak to hand the council (found in wiring, not changed by me):**
the graduated panel copy reads `curve summit reached · graduation: 5 eth class`
while the reconciled canonical target is **4.0 ETH** (WICK-ECONOMY-SPEC §1;
launch record). LAW 4 says no invented numbers; a player who checks the live
meter and then sees "5 eth class" learns that our words drift. Recommend a P2
copy fix through the normal review gate.

## 8. One-line summary for the council

The game already owns an honest compulsion-shaped machine — a shared daily
mountain, a visible peak, a lockable call on tomorrow, an unbuyable scoreboard.
What's missing is the **social and identity rendering of what holders and
streakers already are**: light the vault at the peak, give fire a name and a
season, overlay the community's predictions on the real candle, and let the
rehearsal board show people their own future share. Then the buy is not a
transaction — it is **joining what the player already feels part of.**
