### 5.2 Attribution design (E5.5) — measure the EXIT, not the destination

Constraint [V]: the token page is pad-owned; we cannot read its analytics; the buy event
happens on a page we don't control. So:

- **Outbound (leading indicator):** every bridge link = `/api/go/{placement}` → 302 to
  `https://testnet.vibevibe.fun/token/0xe2ce…216c` with
  `?utm_source=candleclimber&utm_medium={placement}&utm_campaign=pregrad` appended
  (UTMs may or may not surface in pad analytics [A] — they cost nothing). The redirect
  also increments an aggregate Mongo counter `{placement, day, count}` — durable from
  day one (E0.3 closed [V]). No user-level data, no cookies, fail-open on DB error.
  CTR denominator per placement is knowable client-side (runs ended, /airdrop pageviews,
  ready-screen loads).
- **Inbound (referral visit):** share URLs and profile links carry `?r={bucket}` —
  buckets: `dc` (Death Card share, date-coded: `dc-YYYYMMDD` so terrain quality is a
  visible variable), `x` (X bio/posts), `guild` (guild plugs), `gh` (GitHub), `direct`.
  First touch lands in the **board-metadata attribution** field of the identity's first
  weights event (E5.5's "board-metadata attribution"): first-touch-wins, aggregate-only
  reporting (`/api/weights` meta: counts per bucket). No per-user tracking creep;
  walletless visitors counted as anonymous bucket hits only.
- **Outcome truth (the chain):** the ONLY proof of demand is on-chain — curve buys/day
  and net meter delta via the pad API (extends the existing `verify-econ-state.py`
  snapshot [V, E0.4 ritual]). Clicks are leading indicators; correlation across days,
  never claimed as causation. Honest sentence for the weekly review: "X outbound clicks,
  Y referral visits, Z net ETH — consistent with / not consistent with L1 activity."
- **What is platform-native:** Death Card share (in-product CTA + share text),
  weights-board first-touch field, burn-line live number. No third-party trackers
  beyond SimpleAnalytics already in the stack [V — E4.1 mapping].
