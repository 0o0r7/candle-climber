## 2. RESOLVED VERDICTS — the owner's four questions

**Q1 — Bridge product → buy page? YES — bridge the desire moments, as measured attribution, never as a billboard.**
The bridge is outbound-only (the buy happens on the pad, by design), passive-informational,
testnet-stated, no earnings verbs, every click counted via E5.5. Five placements survive
cross-examination (§4): three GREEN ship-able now, two YELLOW gated on owner sign-off +
compliance re-read (Auditor YELLOW-4), one rejected outright. No interstitials, no modals,
no play-adjacent CTAs, nothing inside Death Card content or share text, max **two
buy-adjacent surfaces per session** (Psychologist cap, adopted). Dissent: none on
principle; the Auditor narrows the set and the Chair sides with the Auditor.

**Q2 — Should playing require holding? NO — unanimous, and not close.**
Unlawful under LAW 1.2 (base access free, forever, walletless) and strategically
self-defeating: at a 2% wallet cap, breadth is the only lever that moves the meter; a
hold-wall deletes breadth at the exact step where it is created. The unbuyable scoreboard
is the trust moat and the utility-track case. Holder-gated things live in LAW 2 lanes
only: "holding buys more game — never the game." Auditor's advice stands: do not amend
LAW 1.2. No dissent.

**Q3 — What creates buy-first / buy-more / keep-holding? Belonging → identity → completion.**
Logical: the vault is a visible, named, skill-gated object on today's mountain; holding
is the one-click membership key; the meter is a co-op boss bar a small buy structurally
helps (2% wallet cap = a thousand small holds graduate it). Psychological: "I am a
climber" (free, earned daily) → "climbers who hold carry fire" (visible cosmetic identity)
→ "my play binds to a ledger that matters" (weights via linked wallet) → "holding is how
I stay that person" (identity consistency + daily re-armed vault). Emotional: near-miss at
the peak, pride at the death panel, endowment preview of your own ledger, collective climb
celebration. What must be BUILT: visible holder identity, endowment preview, collection/
seasonal identity, near-miss rendering. Weights stay participation-only — holding never
scales weights (§4, D1).

**Q4 — Upgrade the whole cycle? The rails are plumbing; v2 makes it a cycle.**
Sound: honesty architecture, fee rails, curve-only supply, shipped hooks (weights,
prediction, vault, Merkle rehearsal, burn feed). Missing: the bridge, persistence of
holder identity, the calendar, the reveal ceremony, and any in-product meter. Cycle v2
(§3) closes every arrow with existing or specified mechanisms, phased [NOW]/[AT-GRAD]/
[POST-GRAD], every mechanism law-tagged. Standing finding the council repeats weekly:
**reach, not mechanism, is the binding constraint** — E5 distribution is frozen and
owner-only; unfreezing it is the highest-leverage unlock (Owner Decision Point 4).

## 9. HONESTY BOX — what this cycle will NOT do

- **Not** gate play, retries, submission, leaderboard, duels, or archive browsing on
  holding or a wallet — the base game stays free, forever, walletless (LAW 1.2).
- **Not** put any balance term in any weights formula. No holder weight bonus, no
  multipliers, no "buy-in to boost your airdrop" — unless the owner amends LAW 3.2 with a
  dated note, which the council does not recommend.
- **Not** sell weights or snapshot position; not let holdings touch score, rank, loot, or
  access; not revive a ranked run (LAW 1/3.3).
- **Not** promise profits, yield, airdrops, or "guaranteed" anything. Language stays
  "builds weights", "nothing guaranteed", "eligible — nothing guaranteed". The 75% tax
  share is narrated as the mechanism it is, never as income.
- **Not** use gambling texture: no loot boxes, no wagering, no prize wheels, no
  bet-on-yourself framing; deterministic, disclosed mechanics only.
- **Not** place buy links in score flows, Death Card content or share text, grant
  messages, or anywhere near PLAY/retry; no interstitials, countdowns, or urgency/FOMO.
- **Not** render holder status on the leaderboard (Gold is profile-level only); whales get
  color, never advantage; free climbers are never styled as lesser.
- **Not** invent numbers — surfaces live-read the chain or fail open; docs quote only the
  reconciled evidence trail; every target in this verdict is labeled ASSUMPTION.
- **Not** chase compulsion: no streak terror, no whale-escalation ladders, no spend-pressure
  mechanics — "unmissable daily", never "can't stop".
- **Not** claim pad-volume top-5 this cycle, and **not** buy engagement (bots, reply
  farms) — platform anti-abuse sensitivity is explicit and the credibility cost is
  asymmetric.
