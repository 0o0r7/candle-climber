# TEAM — LOOPBACK

## Ecosystem Researcher
Extracted the founder's actual curation signals: UI/art earns attention, discoverability is a specific critique, builder utility is the dominant personal output, “cook” is the ritual hook, and no-hashtag/cashtag/mention language is the observed dialect. Decision: make the product itself the hook and cite every platform fact.

## Product Designer
Chose a routing puzzle rather than another action game. Decision: first screen must show a playable board and one CTA; three short builds make the loop finishable in a casual scroll session. The product is about shipping a clean route, not collecting a currency.

## Art Director
Chose industrial blueprint / warm terminal rather than dark chart or arena language. Decision: electric violet wires, mint compile state, amber attention, and stamped build reports make the visual identity legible in one screenshot.

## Economy Designer
Chose no token in v0. Decision: optional future `$PATCH` may add blueprint skins, chassis cosmetics, and community boards only; it cannot change ranked time, solve validation, hints, or base access. All future on-chain specifics are marked `[UNVERIFIED — NEEDS OWNER INPUT]`.

## Gameplay Engineer
Owns deterministic 4×4 routing boards, click/tap rotation, keyboard controls, live path validation, three-board sessions, win/lose/retry, local best persistence, and zero external dependencies. Decision: static vanilla HTML/CSS/JS for a low-risk deployable artifact.

## QA / Launch Editor
Owns the stranger test: no documentation required to start, one board solvable quickly, three sessions finish without a dead end, mobile touch is usable, and every copy line respects testnet/no-value/no-reward red lines.
