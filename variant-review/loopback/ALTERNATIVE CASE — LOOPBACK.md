# ALTERNATIVE CASE — LOOPBACK

## Would the founder stop scrolling?

**Likely, if the first frame is shipped as shown:** a large live blueprint board, a bright `START BUILD` button, and the line “Wire the agent. Ship the build.” The idea is legible from a screenshot and speaks to builder culture without requiring the reader to know a token, a chart, or a lore system.

The strongest founder-specific evidence is that UI/art earned praise for Vibe Arena while discoverability was criticized. LOOPBACK puts the game board itself above the fold and makes the first action visually unavoidable. (Source basis: `research/vibevibe/2026-10-03-vibe-team-intel.md`, §3.)

## Platform fit

**Good for a Product & Utility listing:** it is a functioning, free, browser tool-game with a clear use metaphor and no dependency on speculative claims. The launchpad's minimum is an MVP, demo, prototype, or usable flow; LOOPBACK is a usable flow that can be tested immediately. (Source: `docs/TOKEN-LAUNCHPAD-RESEARCH.md`, §3.)

**Good for the founder dialect:** one product hook, a playable URL, a direct `@vibevibefun` mention when eventually posted, and no hashtag wall. The source research records “cook” as the ecosystem ritual and cashtags as the platform's distribution vocabulary. (Source: `research/vibevibe/2026-10-03-vibe-team-intel.md`, §8.)

**Safe under the rails:** v0 does not connect a wallet, read balances, custody anything, or promise rewards. Any future token is explicitly optional and would need current config verification, owner approval, and a separate launch decision. Testnet honesty and “nothing guaranteed” language remain mandatory. (Sources: `research/vibevibe/2026-10-03-vibe-team-intel.md`, §7; `docs/WICK-LAUNCH-FORM-PACK.md`, §8.)

## Distinctness

LOOPBACK is not a derivative of either observed product. It does not climb, jump, scroll vertically, use candle terrain, steer lanes, fight, collect gear, or use holder tiers as the first-run hook. The interaction is a spatial transformation puzzle: the player changes orientation, watches a path compile, and learns the board's logic.

## Retention potential

The first retention hook is mastery: each run is short, deterministic, and readable. The second is a clean build report: a player can improve time, compile streak, and hint-free completion. The third is content extensibility: new board families, agent personalities, and challenge boards can be authored without changing the core.

The honest limitation is that local-only persistence is weaker than a server-backed daily board or community leaderboard. A future version could add a verified board of compile times, but only after anti-abuse and provenance are designed. Do not imply a global ranking in v0.

## Economy honesty

The strongest decision is **no token at v0**. The ecosystem research shows holder-tier utility can be a workable template, but it also shows legal constraints: testnet items have no monetary value, recognition is discretionary, and no reward is promised. A token before the product proves repeat use would make LOOPBACK look like a token wrapper.

If the owner later wants a token, $PATCH should only add visual identity and community boards. It must not change solve validation, ranked time, hint rules, or base access. Every contract address, tax structure, pair, graduation assumption, and participation-weight rule remains **[UNVERIFIED — NEEDS OWNER INPUT]**.

## Where LOOPBACK would lose to the existing flagship

- **Market-native identity:** Candle Climber has a stronger one-sentence claim because the chart itself becomes terrain. LOOPBACK's agent-builder metaphor is ecosystem-native but not market-native.
- **Data depth:** Candle Climber can turn real historical data into a long-lived archive; LOOPBACK's authored boards require content production.
- **Competitive/social proof:** the flagship has a mature concept of score, ghosts, rivals, and share cards. LOOPBACK v0 has only local persistence and a build report.
- **Visual spectacle:** the flagship's custom renderer, weather, characters, and Death Cards are a larger art surface than this intentionally small prototype.
- **Token narrative:** $WICK already has a researched utility roadmap; LOOPBACK's refusal to launch a token in v0 is more honest but less immediately monetizable.

## What must be true for LOOPBACK to win

1. The first board must be playable without reading documentation.
2. The board must be visually satisfying when it compiles; the glow/stamp moment is the product's shareable proof.
3. New boards must arrive often enough that the authored-content weakness is not exposed.
4. A future public score or board must use deterministic validation and anti-abuse controls before being advertised.
5. The launch post must lead with the playable product, not a token thesis.
6. The owner must confirm any future token decision, current chain policy, pair, taxes, and launch copy immediately before signing.

## Confidence and biggest risk

**Credibility confidence: 0.78 / 1.00.** The concept has strong founder-fit, direct first-frame comprehension, and clean red-line compliance. Confidence is not a prediction of curation.

**Biggest risk:** a tiny routing puzzle can feel like a one-minute toy rather than a durable product. The decisive follow-up would be a small authored board pipeline and an honest shared compile-report surface—not token gating.
