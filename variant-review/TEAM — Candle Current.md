# TEAM — Candle Current

## Game Designer
Owns the new interaction contract: a three-lane current-reading game rather than a vertical platformer. Success means a stranger understands “steer toward the calm lane” in ten seconds, can finish a short run, and has a meaningful retry decision. Chose a 24-beat session so the variant can be compared with the original without reproducing its longer systems.

## Art Director
Owns the “instrument panel at sea” direction: ink-black water, cyan chart lines, saffron tape markers, and coral turbulence. Replaced the original’s lime/coral platform language with a quieter cockpit that makes lane state and provenance obvious. Success means the game reads as a different product at a glance while remaining legible on a phone.

## Economy Designer
Owns the separation between rank and recognition. Ranked score is never token-gated; optional $WICK-holder status is described as future/read-only cosmetic access only. Local Signal Notes and streaks are non-transferable progress markers. Success means the economy has a place without compromising fair comparison or implying value.

## Gameplay Engineer
Owns deterministic bundled demo tape, lane transitions, turbulence, boost streaks, input buffering, and local persistence. Deliberately avoided external APIs, wallets, servers, and copied original code so the experiment remains deployable as static hosting. Success means ready → play → win/lose → summary → retry works with keyboard and touch.

## QA Playtester
Owns the definition-of-done pass: first-screen comprehension, three complete sessions, responsive layout, safe reset, and local best persistence. Success means no dead-end state, no dependency on real market data, and explicit “synthetic demo tape” provenance.
